How to Read This Tab
CTR > 5% — excellent. Analyze what worked: face, color contrast, text, emotion.
CTR 2–5% — solid. Look for patterns in what's underperforming.
CTR < 2% — thumbnail isn't converting. A/B test a new version.
Always pair CTR with Avg View Duration — a misleading thumbnail can spike CTR but tank watch time.
